01 / OUR MISSION

Remove barriers between people and languages.

High-quality language learning should not depend on expensive subscriptions or access to a native-speaking tutor. LinguaBridge AI explores how natural synthetic speech and evidence-informed practice can support independent learners.

We begin with Vietnamese for Arabic speakers, emphasizing pronunciation, listening, structured conversation, and gradual confidence building.

A research and development prototype, not yet a commercial service.
